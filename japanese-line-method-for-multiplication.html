Visual Learning

Learn Multiplication Visually

The Japanese Line Method transforms numbers into lines and intersections, helping students understand multiplication clearly and intuitively.

Why this method?

  • • No memorization
  • • Visual understanding
  • • Great for beginners
  • • Builds math confidence

How It Works

Step 1

Draw parallel lines for each digit.

Step 2

Count intersections diagonally.

Step 3

Apply carry-over to get the final answer.
